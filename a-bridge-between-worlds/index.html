Hello, we are Dario and Anna

If something in you is asking to step out of daily life for a while, to see it more clearly and come back to yourself, we invite you into our circles.

We bring together the wisdom of ancestral ritual and the clarity of modern therapeutic tools like Internal Family Systems (IFS). In this space, we guide you to meet the different parts of your inner world with compassion, and to remember the wholeness that has always been inside you.

These experiences make room to let go of what weighs on you, to welcome balance, and to take home practical ways of caring for your heart and spirit every day. What begins in ceremony keeps moving through your life. More presence. More resilience. A deeper connection with yourself and with the people around you.

Our work together

Two ways in.

01

Internal Family Systems

IFS Preparation Circle

In the IFS Preparation Circle we create a safe, supportive space to explore your inner world. Guided by the Internal Family Systems model, you get to meet the different parts of yourself with curiosity and compassion, and begin building a more harmonious relationship with them.

The circle helps you deepen your self-awareness, connect with your intentions, and ease the fears that can come up before stepping into ritual work. Together, we clarify what you are bringing, so you arrive feeling grounded and clear.

The practices we share are not only for the ceremony. They stay with you, as tools you can carry into daily life long after the experience is over.

02

Ceremony

The Chakana Altar Ceremony

The Chakana Altar Ceremony celebrates the interconnection of all life: a bridge between the earth, the cosmos, and the community.

Around this altar, we are invited to honor life as it is, here and now, and to find balance by deepening our connection with ourselves and with the world around us.

In this sacred ritual we gather to heal, to seek clarity, and to strengthen the bonds that hold us together. We look at the health of our relationships and open space for peace, belonging, and renewal.

This ceremony is a doorway. A chance to renew the connection with spirit inside each of us, and to bring that life back into the heart of our community.

Why we do this

At the heart of our work is the meeting of ancient wisdom and modern therapeutic tools, a whole path toward transformation and well-being.

We are here to support people and communities on their own journeys of transformation, with grace, depth, and courage.

Through IFS preparation circles, ceremonies, and other spaces for transformation, we nurture self-discovery, strengthen the bonds of community, and invite a deeper sense of peace and shared purpose.

Who we are

Two paths, one practice.

Dario Hampi Pakari

Psychologist · Kuraka · IFS Practitioner

Dario is a psychologist, a trained IFS Practitioner, and a Kuraka of the Sumak Kawsay tradition. He trained at Sapienza University in Rome and has guided sacred rituals across Europe and the Americas for two decades.

He co-founded the Associazione Fuoco Sacro in Italy and Sumak Kawsay, Iglesia del Buen Vivir, a non-profit based in Florida, US. With the boards of both, he works to keep, support, and share the knowledge of ancient traditions across Europe and the Americas.

With the Fuoco Sacro Council, he has organized the Nina Urku Vision Quest in Italy for fourteen years.

Anna Milaeva

Deep Transformational Coach · IFS

Anna is a certified Deep Transformational Coach who trained in IFS Therapy through Level 3, and a former startup leader. She brings a rich life experience and a steady commitment to inner work and systemic change.

Working with a blend of the IFS model, deep coaching, and breathwork, she supports people and teams on their paths to whole-person well-being, readiness for what is coming, and sustainable growth.

Her coaching helps people explore their inner worlds, find the resources they already carry, and live guided by their true Self.
